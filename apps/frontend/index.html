Why Choose 240–280 GSM Heavyweight Cotton?

Standard high-street fast fashion t-shirts use lightweight 160–180 GSM fabrics that shrink, drape limply, and lose structure after two washes. Bingooo garments use dense 240–280 GSM luxury combed cotton yarn that maintains its structured boxy silhouette, drops squarely off the shoulders, resists wrinkling, and offers all-day breathable comfort tailored for Indian climates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where is Bingooo based and do you deliver across India?

Bingooo is proudly based at 7 Roads Junction, Main Road, Srikakulam, Andhra Pradesh. We ship across all 28 states and 8 union territories in India with express delivery within 3–7 business days.

What does 240 GSM mean?

GSM stands for Grams per Square Metre. A higher GSM indicates a denser, more substantial fabric weave. 240 GSM represents premium heavyweight streetwear cotton that provides the structured, boxy oversized fit popularized in high-end global fashion.

Can I print my own custom artwork on a Bingooo t-shirt?

Yes! Use our interactive 3D Atelier Studio to upload your high-resolution graphics, scale and position them on front or back, and order a one-of-a-kind heavyweight custom tee.
